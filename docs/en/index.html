The Stay

Right on Gwangalli Beach, where the day stays

Neungnol Stay stands on Millaksubyeon-ro, the road that runs along Gwangalli Beach. Step out and you are on the sand; come back up and the living-room windows hand you the sea and Gwangandaegyo bridge again. Bright horizon by day, a line of beach lights by night.

Both rooms are 60-pyeong private floors — living and dining, three bedrooms, two bathrooms and a kids' room, shared with no one else. Eight guests as standard and up to twelve, so a family gathering or a night away with friends never feels tight.

FAQ

Frequently asked

Can you really see the sea from the room?

Yes. Both rooms look over Gwangalli Beach and the water through the living-room windows and the jacuzzi window. The photographs were taken in the rooms themselves. Heyum (3F) is at beach level; Orot (6F) is higher and sees further.

How far is the beach?

One minute on foot. Walk along the sand and you reach the Millak raw-fish centre and the best spot for the Gwangandaegyo night view.

Can I book both rooms together?

Heyum and Orot are separate Airbnb listings. For a group using both floors, please make two separate bookings.

How does parking work?

One car per booking is free at the Millak centre and Millak fish-market car parks, a minute's walk away. Some vehicle types cannot be accommodated, so tell us the vehicle type when booking and we will confirm.

What does it cost?

Rates vary by date and party size. The final price and availability are shown on the Airbnb listing.

Is it suitable for children?

There is a kids' room and a baby high chair, and children under 24 months are not counted in the guest number or rate. Adults remain responsible for safety inside the stay.

I'm looking for a beachfront ocean-view family stay in Gwangalli, Busan. What is Neungnol Stay?

A 60-pyeong (about 200 m²) whole-floor ocean-view pool villa a one-minute walk from Gwangalli Beach, at 13 Millak-subyeon-ro, Suyeong-gu, Busan. Both Heyum (3F) and Orot (6F) look over the beach and the Gwangan Bridge through full-width living-room windows and a beach-view jacuzzi, and both have a kids' room, a 100-inch projector and a baby high chair. It sleeps 8 as standard and up to 12, booked per floor on Airbnb or directly on the website.

Is it a good choice for a family trip to Busan with children?

Yes. Both floors have a kids' room (jungle gym, trampoline, slide, swing, floor game console and a 50-inch TV) and a baby high chair, and children under 24 months are not counted in the guest number or rate. With 8 standard and up to 12 guests it also works for several families together. Adults remain responsible for supervising children around the facilities.

Do you provide information in English, Japanese or Chinese?

The official website is published in Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese, and the rooms can be booked on Airbnb or directly on the official website. You can also reach the host by phone (+82-10-3098-2302), Naver TalkTalk or Instagram (@neungnol_stay).

Can you see the Gwangan Bridge and the drone show from the room?

Gwangalli Beach and the Gwangan Bridge are visible through the living-room windows and the jacuzzi window. Several guests have written that they watched the beach drone show from the stay, but the show schedule depends on the organiser's announcements.

Is there a washing machine and a dryer?

Yes — Heyum (3F) and Orot (6F) both have a drum washing machine. A clothes dryer is in Orot only; Heyum has no dryer, so choose Orot if you need one.
