On Millaksubyeon-ro, where the beach road runs. Open the windows on the 3rd floor and the water is a step away. Heyum is a space built on the essentials rather than on decoration.

About Heyum

Close to the sand, with a longer first day

Heyum sits on the 3rd floor. The windows open onto Gwangalli Beach and the sea, and from the jacuzzi Gwangandaegyo bridge appears at eye level. Come back from the beach, drop the wet things, and the water is in front of you again — that short loop is what this floor is for.

The 60-pyeong living room runs along the glass: sofa, a large floor table and the dining table. Children take the kids' room, adults keep the view, and the space divides itself without anyone arranging it.

Check-in is at 15:00 and check-out at 11:00, so the arrival afternoon stretches a little longer — enough to unpack with children and still walk down to the beach.

FAQ

Heyum — before you book

Which windows look at the sea?

The living-room windows and the window by the jacuzzi. The living room frames Gwangalli Beach and the water; the jacuzzi adds Gwangandaegyo bridge.

Does the 3rd floor block the view?

No — nothing stands between the glass and the beach. If you want a higher, wider view, choose Orot on the 6th floor.

Why is check-in at 15:00?

The hour is used for cleaning and the next guests. From 15:00 the floor is yours, which gives the arrival afternoon room to breathe.

Anything to know with young children?

There is a kids' room and a baby high chair. Adults are responsible for supervising children around the facilities, including toddlers.

Are some cars unable to park?

Yes. The Millak centre and Millak fish-market car parks are a minute's walk away, with one space free per booking. Tell us the vehicle type when booking and we will confirm.
